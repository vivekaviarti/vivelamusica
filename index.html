Chapter I · The artist behind the voice

Music has always felt like home.

My musical journey began in childhood and has grown through years of choral singing and solo performances. I have been part of choirs for around two decades and have sung as a soloist since my early teens.

My heart belongs to expressive music: the reverence of classical and sacred works, the intricacy of vocal ensemble, and the emotional storytelling of musical theatre. Each piece is an invitation to discover another dimension of the human experience.
